Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Calcora ("we," "our," or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our calculator application.

1. Information we collect

We do NOT collect or store any personal information on our servers. All your data stays on your device.

The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Calculation history: Your previous calculations are stored locally on your device for your convenience. We do not have access to this data, and it is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties.
  • Theme preferences: Your selected theme and unlocked premium themes are stored locally using secure encrypted storage.
  • App settings: Your preferences, such as live calculation mode and angle mode (degrees/radians), are saved locally.

2. Third-party services

Calcora uses the following third-party service:

Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the app. AdMob may collect and process certain data to provide personalized ads. This may include:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • Ad interaction data (clicks, impressions)
  • Anonymous identifiers for advertising purposes

For more information about how Google processes data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps

3. Data storage and security

All your personal data (calculation history, theme preferences, settings) is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms:

  • SQLite database: Used for calculation history with local encryption.
  • Secure storage: Theme preferences and settings are stored using expo-secure-store with hardware-backed encryption when available.

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

4. Data sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or transfer your data to third parties. The only data sharing occurs through Google AdMob, as described in the Third-party services section above.

5. Children's privacy

Calcora does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is designed to be safe for all ages and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Your rights and choices

You have full control over your data:

  • Clear history: You can delete your calculation history at any time from within the app.
  • Reset themes: Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data.
  • Ad choices: You can manage ad personalization through your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads).

7. Data retention

Data is retained locally on your device until you:

  • Manually clear your calculation history
  • Uninstall the application
  • Clear the app's data through your device settings

8. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Displaying a notice in the app (for significant changes)

Your continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

9. International users

Calcora is available worldwide. If you are using the app from outside your home country, please note that your data (stored locally on your device) remains subject to the laws of your jurisdiction.

10. Do Not Track

Our app does not track users across third-party websites. However, Google AdMob may track ad interactions as described in their privacy policy.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us:

Email: support.calcora@gmail.com

We will respond to your inquiry within 48 hours.
